After four decades running and overseeing hotels for the world's largest brands, Stephen Ayers kept meeting the same person: an owner with a good property, no professional operator, and no one to call who wasn't priced for a 300-room hotel. Ayers Hospitality exists for that owner.

Who you'll work with

Partner - hotel operations

Stephen W. Ayers

A cum laude graduate of the Lausanne hotel school, Stephen has spent over forty years managing hotels in England, Israel, South Africa, Cyprus, Germany and Canada. General manager at thirty-three, he has opened multiple hotels, built a chain from a single property to six, and served as Director of Asset Management over a portfolio that included the Fairmont Château Frontenac, the Fairmont Queen Elizabeth, the Fairmont Château Laurier, the Fairmont Washington DC and two Hilton properties in Toronto. He now works only with independent properties.

Managing director - client lead, Porto

William Ayers

William grew up in the hotel business and spent his early career in commercial real estate and asset management in Toronto, overseeing budgets, leasing and on-site operations across a portfolio of properties. He later founded and ran a multi-service retail business for seven years. Based in Porto, William is your first point of contact, leads the commercial and marketing side of every engagement, and is on site for the Health Check and programme work.
