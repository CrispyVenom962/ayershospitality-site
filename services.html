Services and pricing

Every package is a fixed price agreed before we start. No day rates, no hourly billing, no surprises. Prices exclude VAT and are scoped to the size of your business. Travel outside the Porto area is charged at cost.

Operational Health Check

from €950smaller properties · larger properties from €1,500

One day on site. We walk every area of the property, sit with you and your team, and go through the numbers. Within a week you receive a written report and a 90-day action plan you can execute yourself or with us.

  • Occupancy, rates and revenue per room
  • Booking.com and direct-booking mix
  • Online reputation and review handling
  • Staffing, rotas and payroll cost
  • Food and beverage, housekeeping, purchasing
  • Competitor positioning in your area
The starting point for almost every client.

90-Day Improvement Programme

from €3,900smaller properties · larger properties from €5,500

We take the action plan and implement it with your team over three months: weekly on-site or remote sessions, pricing changes, procedures, supplier reviews and a monthly results report to you as owner.

  • Weekly management meeting with you and your team
  • Pricing and channel changes, done, not just recommended
  • Standard procedures for front desk and housekeeping
  • Review response and reputation routine
  • Cost review of top suppliers
  • Monthly owner report: occupancy, rate, profit
For owners who want the problems fixed, not described.

Part-Time General Manager

from €2,500/monthone day a week on site plus remote · two days from €4,000/month

An experienced general manager working inside your business on a part-time basis. Ongoing, month to month after a three-month minimum. A fraction of the cost of employing a senior GM.

  • Runs the weekly management rhythm
  • Owns revenue, costs and quality standards
  • Coaches your head of reception and housekeeping
  • Handles suppliers, contracts and renewals
  • Available by phone between visits
  • Monthly reporting to you as owner
For businesses that need a GM but can't justify one full time.

Pre-Opening Management

from €8,000project fee · scoped to the property

From building site to stabilised operation: staffing plan, recruitment, procedures, property management system, channel setup, pricing, suppliers, training and opening.

  • Recruitment and training
  • PMS, channel manager and OTA setup
  • Launch pricing and revenue strategy
  • Soft opening and first 90 days
For first-time hotel and restaurant owners, and developers.
