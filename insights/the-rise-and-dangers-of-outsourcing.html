The rise and dangers of outsourcing

By Stephen Ayers · 1 min read

Outsourcing has been around for years. Used wisely, it’s a smart tool. Hotels outsource linen, laundry, even food service - and that can work perfectly well.

But outsourcing staff? That’s where things get dangerous.

I saw this firsthand in a hotel in Germany. On paper, agency staff seem like the perfect solution. Need people tomorrow? One phone call. Don’t need them next week? No problem.

But here’s what really happens.

Your loyal employees notice agency staff earn more per hour. Morale drops. Agency workers rotate daily - hard to train, no loyalty, inconsistent service. Standards slip. Complaints rise. Costs increase.

Soon your best people leave to join the agencies. Now you depend even more on temporary staff. That’s the vicious cycle.

Service suffers. Reputation declines. Returning guests disappear.

Outsourcing staff isn’t the solution to every problem. Used occasionally? Yes. As a habit? No.

What truly differentiates a hotel isn’t the building. It’s loyal, long-term staff who know your guests, your standards, your culture.

Turn your back on them - and you risk losing everything that makes your hotel thrive.
