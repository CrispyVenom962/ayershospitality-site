60 Seconds with Stephen · #02

The importance of behaviour

By Stephen Ayers · 1 min read

Many years ago, I read a piece of advice that shaped my entire career.

When you start out, be neutral.

Don’t be the revolutionary. Don’t be the teacher’s pet.Be a face in the crowd - learning, observing, absorbing.

Think of your career like a bubble in a glass of sparkling water. At first, it sits quietly on the side of the glass. Then, slowly - naturally - it rises.

That’s how advancement should happen.

When you begin, respect your bosses and colleagues. Look. Listen. Learn. Offer opinions when asked. Work hard. Learn harder.

Make mistakes - everyone who works makes them. But never hide them. Dishonesty leaves a stain that competence cannot remove.

Don’t rush promotion. Earn it.

If you work with integrity and humility, your effort will be noticed.

And one day - your bubble will rise.
